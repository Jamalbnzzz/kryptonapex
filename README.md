# ZenScout APEX — Localhost Solana Trading Terminal ⚡

A high-performance, private, web-based trading dashboard designed to run 100% locally on your own PC (`http://localhost:3000`). It connects directly to your Phantom Wallet and features autonomous execution across 4 distinct trading strategies with automated profit sweeping to your cold storage vault.

---

## 🌟 Key Features

1. **Cyberpunk Trading Dashboard UI:**
   - Dark mode, glassmorphism design optimized for low-latency decision making.
   - 1-click **Connect Phantom** integration (`window.solana`).
   - Real-time SOL balance display and terminal console ticker.

2. **4 Autonomous Trading Modes:**
   - **Mode 1: 46K MC Breakout Sniper:** Targets tokens breaking out at ~$46K Market Cap (60% curve completion). Avoids 4K dev rugs and rides momentum to 69K+ graduation.
   - **Mode 2: Copy-Trader (Spy Wallet):** Input any target trader's address; mirrors their buys and sells on-chain in real-time.
   - **Mode 3: Whale & Syndicate Tracker:** Monitors verified Master Funders (>100 SOL balance) and pre-arms trades before launch.
   - **Mode 4: Smart Math Exit & Preemptive Whale Defense:** Exits at $62K MC graduation wall, front-runs whale sells (>2 SOL dumps), +45% derisk, -18% stop-loss.

3. **🛡️ Automated Cold Wallet Profit Sweep:**
   - Keep a designated reserve (e.g. `0.05 SOL`) in your active trading bot.
   - Any profits exceeding this threshold are automatically transferred on-chain to your main cold/savings wallet every 30 seconds!

4. **🔒 100% Private Local Execution:**
   - Runs exclusively on `localhost` (127.0.0.1).
   - No external websites, no cloud storage, no exposure to other users.

## 🚀 Quick Start Guide (Dalil l-khedma b Darija)

### Khatwa 1: Lansi l-Terminal
F Windows (PC dyalek):
* Double-clik 3la **`START_DASHBOARD.bat`**.
* Ghay-7el lik automatique f l-navigateur (Edge aw Chrome):
  `http://localhost:3000`

### Khatwa 2: Rbet Phantom Wallet
* Cliqui 3la **Connect Phantom** l-fouq 3la limn.
* Ghay-tla3 lik l-balance dyalek d SOL f blast-ha.

### Khatwa 3: Khtar l-Mode w l-Mablagh
1. Khtar wahed mn les 4 modes:
   - **Mode 1:** 46K MC Sniper (Moyenne d 60% bonding curve).
   - **Mode 2:** Copy-Trader (Colli CA d chi trader).
   - **Mode 3:** Whale Tracker (Master Funders).
   - **Mode 4:** Smart Exit (Preemptive Defense).
2. Khtar l-mablagh d kol sef9a (mthal: `0.01 SOL` aw `0.05 SOL`).
3. Activi **Auto-Sweep Profits** w 7ot l'adresse d l-wallet l-khasa dyalek bach ay rb7 zayed y-t7wel liha automatique.
4. Cliqui 3la **ACTIVATE LOCAL ENGINE**.
